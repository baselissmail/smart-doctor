# Getting help

- **"How do I…?" or "Is this expected?"** → [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/baselissmail/smart-doctor/discussions).
- **smart-doctor named the wrong cause** → open a [Wrong diagnosis](https://github.com/baselissmail/smart-doctor/issues/new/choose) issue with your `--report` output. These reports are how it gets smarter.
- **Something is broken** → open a [Bug](https://github.com/baselissmail/smart-doctor/issues/new/choose) issue.
- **Security problem** → report it privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before you post anything:

- Share `smart-doctor … --report` output: it masks the client ID, patient IDs, tokens, codes and secrets.
- **Never post private keys, client secrets, tokens, authorization codes or patient data**, and leave out anything that identifies a customer or organisation.
- The [error catalogue](https://baselissmail.github.io/smart-doctor/errors/) explains every cause smart-doctor can report, with fixes.

smart-doctor is maintained in spare time; there's no guaranteed response time, but every well-described report gets read.
